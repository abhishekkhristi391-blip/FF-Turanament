I Love You, Anushka 💙

Aaj Girlfriend Day hai, aur maine pehli baar tere liye kuch banaya hai. Perfect nahi hoga, kahin koi galti bhi ho sakti hai — but sab kuch dil se banaya hai, to please galti mat dekhna, bas mera pyaar dekhna. 💙

Sabne tujhe sirf istemal kiya aur chhod diya, kisi ne bhi tujhe sahi maayne mein sambhal ke nahi rakha. But I promise — jab tak tu mere saath hai, main aisa kabhi hone nahi dunga. Aur kabhi khud ko akela mat samajhna, ye sochkar ki koi tere saath nahi hai — main hamesha tere saath hoon, bas meri body thodi door hai.

Mujhe pata hai itni door rehke kaisa lagta hai, aur mujhe achha lagta hai ki itni door rehkar bhi tu mujh par itna trust karti hai. I trust you too — bas kabhi-kabhi ek dar sa rehta hai, ki kahin koi tujhe mujhse door na le jaye. Isi dar ki wajah se kabhi-kabhi hamare beech jhagda ho jaata hai, uske liye sorry. Par jis din hum ek ho jayenge, ye sare dar khud hi door ho jayenge.

Mujhe ek dar hamesha rehta hai — ki agar future mein maine achhi job na payi, ya kuch bhi na kar paya, to kya hoga? Main tere mummy-papa ke saamne kaise aaunga? Bas yehi sochkar dar lagta hai, ki kahin main tere layak na ban paaun. I love you, forever and always.
— Abhishek
